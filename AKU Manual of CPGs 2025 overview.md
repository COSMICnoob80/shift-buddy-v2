## About the Manual

The AKU Manual of Clinical Practice Guidelines represents a watershed moment in standardising clinical care across Pakistan. Developed meticulously over four years, this comprehensive resource aims to improve patient outcomes through the integration of global best practices **tailored to local contexts**. It provides uniform, evidence‑based clinical guidelines for **140 of the most common and significant diseases** in Pakistan, covering approximately **80% of conditions seen in healthcare institutions** across the country. The manual is expected to directly improve healthcare outcomes for **over 21 million people** in Pakistan and potentially avert an estimated **280,000 deaths annually**.

> *“This manual is more than a guide; it is a movement to transform healthcare in Pakistan. With nearly 140 guidelines addressing 80% of conditions seen in healthcare institutions in the country, this initiative is a giant leap toward ensuring every patient receives high‑quality care.”*  
> — Dr. Adil Haider, Editor‑in‑Chief

## Methodology: GRADE‑ADOLOPMENT

The AKU Manual was developed using **GRADE‑ADOLOPMENT**, an innovative methodology that combines three approaches for formulating clinical recommendations:

| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Adoption** | Using source guideline recommendations as‑is or with minor changes |
| **Adaptation** | Modifying recommendations based on local context using **Evidence‑to‑Decision (EtD) tables** |
| **De‑novo creation** | Creating entirely new recommendations where no suitable source guideline exists |

This methodology, employed in collaboration with the **U.S. GRADE Working Group**, marks the **first use of GRADE‑ADOLOPMENT in Pakistan** and establishes AKU as the **first officially recognized GRADE Centre in South Asia**.

## Comparison: AKU Manual vs. Doctor‑On‑Duty Guide

| Feature | AKU Manual of CPGs | Doctor‑On‑Duty Guide (Dr. Asif Ali Khan) |
|---------|-------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Methodology** | GRADE‑ADOLOPMENT (adoption, adaptation, de‑novo) | Not formally graded; based on clinical experience and textbooks |
| **Development** | 4 years, 150+ faculty, multi‑institutional collaboration | Single‑author with contributions from colleagues |
| **Evidence Base** | Systematic reviews, EtD tables, U.S. GRADE Network endorsement | Practical, experience‑based; references standard textbooks |
| **Scope** | 140 guidelines, 3+ volumes, adult + paediatric + surgical | 193 topics covering OPD, emergency, ward skills, neuro‑psychiatry, dermatology, surgery |
| **Target Audience** | All healthcare sectors (primary to tertiary); nationwide standardisation | House officers, medical officers, interns, trainees |
| **Format** | Web app, mobile app, printed volumes; bilingual potential | PDF / printed booklet |
| **Access** | Free via app/web; printed copies for purchase | Free PDF; printed via self‑publishing |
| **Updates** | Bi‑annual updates through CCBP | Periodic new editions (1st ed. 2021, 2nd ed. 2022) |
